  1. The gas tank of a parked pickup truck develops a leak. The amount of gas, in liters, remaining in the tank after t hours is represented by the function $V(t) = 90{\left( {1 - \frac{{t}}{{18}}} \right)^2}\quad 0 \le t \le 18$. How fast is the gas leaking from the tank after 12 hours?

    How to get the Solution: $f(x) = 90 - 10t + \frac{{5}}{{18}}{t^2}$

    $f'(x) = - 10 + \frac{{5}}{{9}}t$

    $f'(12) = - 3.33$

    Answer to Problem: The gas is leaking from the tank at a rate of 3.33 liters per hour after 12 hours.

  2. Andrew and David are training to run a marathon. They both go on a run on Sunday mornings at precisely 7 A.M. Andrew’s house is 22 km south of David’s. One Sunday morning, Andrew leaves his house and runs west at 7 km/hr. The distance between the two runners can be modeled by the function $$s(t) = \sqrt {130{t^2} - 396t + 484},$$ where s is in kilometers and t is in hours. Determine the rate at which the distance between the two runners is changing.

    How to get the Solution: $s(t)={{\left( 130{{{t}}^{{2}}}-396t+484 \right)}^{{}^{{1}}/{}_{{2}}}}$

    $s'(t)=\frac{{1}}{{2}}{{\left( 130{{{t}}^{{2}}}-396t+484 \right)}^{{}^{-1}/{}_{{2}}}}(260t-396)$

    Answer to Problem: $s'(t)=\frac{260t-396}{2\sqrt{130{{{t}}^{{2}}}-396t+484}}$

General Derivative Rules Using the Chain Rule

$ \frac d{{dx}}\left[f(x)\right]^n=n\left[f(x)\right]^{n-1}\cdot f'(x)$

$\frac d{{dx}}\ln\left[f(x)\right]=\frac1{f(x)}\cdot f'(x)$

$\frac d{{dx}}e^{f(x)}=e^{f(x)}\cdot f'\left(x\right)​$

    Finding the Equation of the Tangent Line
    1. Find the y value by calculating $f(a)$: $\left(a, f(a)\right)$
    2. Find the slope of the tangent line by calculating $f'(a)$: ${m_{{tan}}}=f'(a)$
    3. Equation of the tangent line: $y-f(a)=f'(a)\left(x-a\right)$
    Finding the Value(s) where the Tangent Line is Horizontal
    1. Set $f'(x)=0$
    2. Solve for $x$
    3. Verify that each $x$ is in the domain of $f(x)$ and $f'(x)$
