Class Introduction / Using the Discussion Tool

Post Your Introduction

As we discussed on the previous page, the "discussions" tool (found under the "Course Work" navigation bar menu) provides a valuable forum for learner to learner and learner to instructor interactions. It is now your turn to use the discussion board.

  1. Select the "Discussions" tool from the "Course Tools" course menu
  2. Click on Introduce Yourself under the Class Introductions forum
  3. Click on compose
  4. Tell the class your name and anything else you would like them to know. Remember, Netiquette rules do apply.
