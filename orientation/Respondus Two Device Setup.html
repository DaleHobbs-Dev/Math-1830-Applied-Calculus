Respondus Monitor and LockDown Browser With Two Devices

Setup instructions for a valid at-home proctoring session.

Important: Read Before You Begin!

Please note that the Respondus Monitoring software employed by this course requires the simultaneous use of TWO devices:

  1. A computer (with a working web camera) that you plan to complete the exam with.
  2. A SECOND web-connected device capable of recording video (like a smartphone or a laptop). This second device must be able to scan a QR code and connect to the internet.

Keep both devices connected to power

It is incredibly important that both devices are plugged into an outlet for the entire duration of the exam.

To ensure your exam session is valid and to avoid technical flags, please follow the monitoring setup instructions with the same level of seriousness as the exam itself. Read through the following guide before you take the exam, so you have an idea of what to expect.

To Start the Practice Exam

  • Navigate to your Pearson MyLabs assignments and find the "Practice Exam" that will help give you an idea how to set up Respondus for at-home testing.
    • Note: if you are testing this semester using an in-person testing facility, message your instructor directly letting them know this.
  • If you do not already have it installed, you'll need to download and install Pearson's "Lockdown Browser" before you are able to take the exam.
  • When you start the quiz, the required proctoring software will launch.
    • The monitoring software requires a Web Camera and will go through several start-up steps before opening the quiz.

Monitoring Setup Steps

Step One: Identification and Materials Verification

  • Hold your student ID directly in front of the camera so that it fills the frame and all text is legible.
  • If the start-up task asks you to, please hold up your formula sheet or permitted resources to the camera so both sides are clearly visible, and the content is readable.
  • Avoid Poor Framing: Do not hold your ID too far back or at an angle where the camera cannot capture the details.

Step Two: Comprehensive Workspace Scan

  • Conduct a full sweep of your workspace.
  • When prompted to scan your environment, move your camera slowly to show your entire workspace. This includes the desk surface, your calculator, any permitted physical resources, and the immediate area around your chair.
  • Do not simply wiggle the camera back and forth or focus on unrelated items like pets—even if they are adorable. A "micro-scan" that only shows the space directly behind your monitor is insufficient.

Step Three: Maintain Main Camera Presence During the Exam

  • Throughout the exam, ensure your entire face and shoulders are visible to your main webcam.
  • Check Your Angle: A main camera angle that only captures your forehead or the ceiling fan will result in an invalid session. The camera should be angled slightly downward from the top of your monitor to capture you and your immediate workspace.

Step Four: Correctly Set Up the Required Respondus Monitor SECOND Camera

  • The Respondus Monitor setup will require you to set up a SECOND camera. This camera must be a separate device that can be positioned to show your hands and workspace during the duration of the exam.
  • It is very important that your device be connected to power for the duration of the exam. Failure to maintain a full second recording will result in an invalid proctoring session and an invalid exam attempt. Do not forget to plug in your device!
  • It is highly recommended that your second device is connected directly to WiFi (or Ethernet), rather than relying on wireless data (the device will be recording a video feed of your exam).
  • Follow the steps at startup to scan a QR code on your second device to link your recording to your monitoring session.
  • Please make sure that your secondary camera is oriented so that it can record your full workspace (hands, keyboard, mouse, worksurface) at all times during the exam. If your workspace is not fully visible or not recorded by the secondary camera for the entire exam, the exam will be considered invalid. Your secondary camera view should look like the "Do This" panel in the reference image below. A setup like "Don't Do This" would be considered invalid because your workspace and hands are not visible throughout the exam.

Take time to check the camera view

There isn't a timer for setting this part up, so take your time and make sure your recording setup is stable, plugged in, and meets the expectations (and there's nothing more disappointing than realizing you had the camera flipped around incorrectly the whole time, so your entire exam is void).

Consequences of Improper Setup

Failure to follow these steps may result in the following:

  • You may be required to take a different version of the exam in person with a live proctor.
  • You may be asked to complete a supplemental spoken exam with the instructor to verify your results.
  • Points can be deducted from exams for improper room scans.
  • You may receive a zero on the exam.
