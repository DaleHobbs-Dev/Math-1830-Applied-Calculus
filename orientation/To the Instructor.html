Start-of-Semester Email for Instructors

A sample welcome message for Math 1830

Before class starts

It is wise to send a beginning-of-the-semester email. Here is one I commonly use. You are welcome to edit it as needed.

Before sending

Update the term, year, instructor name, Remind course code, and Remind join link. Review any details about assignments and proctoring for your section.

Email subject

Welcome to Spring/Fall/Summer 20XX: Getting Started Guide to Math 1830!

Email body

Hello!

I'm your instructor for Math 1830 (Applied/Business Calculus), [your name], and I wanted to give you some basic information for starting the class tomorrow and throughout this week. This is an online course, so look below for the links to get started. Below is the link for Online Campus (bookmark it), as well as information about the Remind app that I use to communicate with students throughout the semester. You should have access to Online Campus starting at midnight tonight!

elearn.columbiastate.edu is the direct link to Online Campus. It's a good idea to log in and start going through the “Start Here” module this week, beginning tomorrow. It will help you become familiar with the course. Be sure to review the syllabus and how grading works for the various aspects of the course. You can also send an email through Online Campus, although I usually prefer texting through Remind.

Once you get into Online Campus, make it your goal to complete these activities from the “Start Here” module this week:

  • Join the Remind app, either through the app on your phone or by texting the course code @XXXXX to 81010. On a mobile device, you can also use the Remind join link. Remind is my main way to communicate and send quick announcement reminders, so you will need access. If you need help, watch this video tutorial for joining Remind.
  • Read the syllabus and go through all the items in the “Start Here” module of Online Campus.
  • Post an introduction for yourself on the discussion board.
  • Complete the “Start Here” module quiz. Assignments can be found under the “MyLab Math” content section in Online Campus. You get three tries on quizzes, so hopefully you earn 100% on at least one attempt of the orientation quiz. Your highest score will be saved.
  • Try the “Sample Test” to become familiar with using your webcam and the Pearson LockDown Browser on your computer. This is how remote proctoring works, but you also have the option to test in person on a campus if you do not have access to a webcam.
  • Later in the week, start your assignments, beginning with the two review homework sections.

If you have any questions, feel free to text me in the Remind app or message me in Online Campus. Let me know if you need help getting started this week!
